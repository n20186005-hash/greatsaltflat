# Documentary photographs

Each file was compared byte-for-byte with its Commons original before resizing on 23 October 2026. Source warnings and licence sections were checked individually.

- `public/media/salinas-grandes-horizon-ccby-sa3.jpg`: Gonzalo Rivero, 28 December 2008, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Salinas_grandes.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `public/media/ruta-52-cuesta-lipan-ccby-sa3.jpg`: Gonzalo Rivero, 29 December 2008, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ruta_52-cuesta_lip%C3%A1n.jpg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- `public/media/salinas-grandes-pools-ccby2.jpg`: Guerretto, 24 September 2007, [source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Salinas_grandes_(Salta_y_Jujuy).jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

Shipped versions are resized to maximum 1600px and JPEG quality80; CSS crops occur on display. Adaptations retain the corresponding licence. Per-file original URLs, dates, hashes and processing records are in `src/lib/photos.json`; authors, original links, licence links and changes are also visible in both languages. Historical photographs do not establish today’s conditions or the exact visitor gate.

The existing illustrated brand mark is separate from documentary photography. Unverified photoreal support images were removed, including their unused source copies.
